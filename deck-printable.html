Why Current Workarounds Fail
  • Workaround 1: High-Fee Consultancies (ANSR, Zinnov, Big-4): Retainers of $100k–$300k. Incentive conflict: advisors often take undisclosed kickbacks from commercial landlords and preferred staffing vendors.
  • Workaround 2: WhatsApp Whisper Networks: Ex-pat leaders ask informal WhatsApp groups ("Who handled your incorporation in Bangalore?"). Subjective, unvetted, and non-standardized.
  • Workaround 3: Annual Macro PDF Reports (NASSCOM, CBRE, JLL): Aggregate numbers with zero operational utility (no real-time salary benchmarks for an ML Lead in Hyderabad in Q4 2026). 12–18 months outdated upon release.
  • Workaround 4: Manual MCA & ROC Filings: Government legal filings show corporate registrations, but contain zero tech telemetry, tech stacks, or engineering maturity context.
